Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This notice explains, in plain terms, how NovaWatch handles your information.

What we collect

When you create a NovaWatch account, we collect the information needed to set up and run that account, such as your email address and the sign-in details provided by your chosen sign-in method (for example, Google Sign-In). We also store the watchlist data you build inside the app — the movies and TV shows you add, your watch progress, and your preferences — so it's there the next time you sign in.

What we use it for

We use your information for one purpose only: to create and operate your NovaWatch account and to provide the app's features to you (such as syncing your library across devices and sending release alerts you've opted into). We do not use your information for anything else — no advertising, no selling or sharing it with third parties for marketing, and no profiling beyond what's needed to run the app itself.

Third-party services

NovaWatch retrieves publicly available movie and TV metadata from The Movie Database (TMDB) to display titles, posters, and related information. This is a one-way lookup of show/movie data — it is not linked to your personal account information.

Deleting your account

If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email us at delete@novawatch.site and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If you have any questions about this notice or how your information is handled, email info@novawatch.site.
